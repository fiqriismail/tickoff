## Add a task

1. Select **Inbox**, another Tasks project, or **All Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Type a title into the quick-add field above the list.
3. Choose **Low**, **Medium** or **High** priority.
4. Press **Return** or choose **Add**.

A new task starts as **To Do**. A title containing only spaces cannot be added.
Priority starts at Medium; the quick-add bar keeps your chosen priority for the
next task.
**File > New Task** (**Command-N**) focuses the quick-add field; it does not open a sheet.

Inside a project, new tasks belong to that project. Under **All Projects**, the
project menu offers Inbox, recent projects and your other active Tasks projects.
After adding a task, that choice returns to Inbox under All Projects, or to the
selected project when viewing one project. Daily Goals and archived projects are
not quick-add destinations.

## Edit details or move a task

Double-click a row, select it and press **Return**, or choose **Task > Edit Task**
(**Command-E**). You can also Control-click a row and choose **Edit**.

Change the title, notes, status, priority or project, then choose **Save**.
Choose **Cancel** or press **Escape** to discard changes. Pressing Return in the
title field saves.

The **Project** picker moves the task to another active Tasks project. If its
current project is archived, that project remains in the picker so you can keep
the task there or move it out.

## Status and completion

Tasks have three statuses: **To Do**, **In Progress** and **Done**.

- Click the circle beside an unfinished task to mark it Done.
- Click a completed task's circle to return it to To Do.
- To choose In Progress or another status explicitly, Control-click the row and
  use **Change Status**, or select it and use **Task > Mark As**.

Completing a task does not delete it. Done tasks have a struck-through title
and appear after unfinished tasks. Within each group, tasks are ordered by
priority, then due date (undated tasks last), then newest first.

## Due dates

In the editor, turn on **Due date** and choose a calendar date. Turn it off to
remove the date.

Unfinished tasks show **Today**, **Tomorrow**, or a short date. Dates before today
show **Overdue** with the date. Completed tasks show the date without an overdue
warning. A due date is not a timed reminder or notification.

## Delete a task

Select a row and choose **Task > Delete** (**Command-Delete**), or Control-click
it and choose **Delete**. The list also handles **Delete** while it has keyboard focus.

:::danger[Deletion is immediate]
Deleting a single task has no confirmation dialog, Trash or app-level recovery.
Mark it Done instead if you want to keep it. A saved
[backup](../../help/data-and-backups/) is the recovery option after deletion.
:::

## Clear completed tasks

Choose **File > Clear Completed** or **Clear Completed** in the toolbar's
**More** menu. Review the number of tasks and confirm.

This permanently removes **all Done tasks in the selected project**, or all
Tasks projects under All Projects, including archived projects' tasks. It is not
limited to the rows matching a search or status filter. Daily Goals progress is
not affected. The command is disabled when there is nothing to clear.
