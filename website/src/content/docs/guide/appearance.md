## Follow your Mac

Tickoff follows macOS light/dark appearance and accent colour. Change these in
**System Settings > Appearance**. There is no in-app theme, accent or text-size
preference and no separate Settings window.

The documentation website's light/dark selector affects only the website, not
the Mac app.

## Show or hide notes

In a Tasks view, choose **View > Show Notes**, or open the toolbar's **More**
ellipsis menu and toggle **Show Notes**.

When enabled, each task shows a one-line note preview. Hiding previews does not
delete notes, and [search](../search-and-filters/#search-titles-and-notes) still
matches their full text. Open the task editor to read or change the complete note.

## Use compact rows

Choose **View > Use Compact Rows**, or the same option in **More**. Compact rows
reduce spacing and put the title and metadata together. Show Notes remains a
separate option.

Both display options are saved locally and apply across task views. They do
not change the dedicated Daily Goals layout; their commands are disabled there.

## Adjust the sidebar

Use the sidebar toolbar button or **Control-Command-S** to hide/show it.
Drag its divider to resize it within the supported width.

Tickoff uses one main window. Closing that window quits the app; reopen Tickoff
to continue with your saved data.

See also [Keyboard shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts/).
