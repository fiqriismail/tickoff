Tickoff keeps your tasks, projects and daily goals on your Mac. There is no account
to create, and your data is stored locally.

## Start here

- [Install Tickoff and add your first task](./getting-started/).
- [Get help with installation and first launch](../help/troubleshooting/).
- [Download the latest release](https://github.com/fiqriismail/tickoff/releases/latest).

## Two ways to organise your day

**Tasks projects** hold tasks with notes, status, priority and optional due dates.
Use **Inbox** as a starting point, or create a project to group related work.

**Daily Goals projects** have a dedicated Today view for daily check-offs and
whole-number counters. Progress starts fresh each local calendar day.

Tickoff requires **macOS 14 Sonoma or later** and supports Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
